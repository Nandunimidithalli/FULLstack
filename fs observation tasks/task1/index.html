Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS)

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) is one of the leading engineering colleges in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. The college was established in 2001 and is affiliated with Andhra University. It offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and technology.

Courses Offered

  1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  2. Computer Science and Engineering (CSM)
  3. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  4. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
  5. Mechanical Engineering
  6. Civil Engineering

Facilities Available

  • Central Library
  • Computer Laboratories
  • Sports Complex
  • Hostel Facility
  • Wi-Fi Campus
  • Cafeteria
